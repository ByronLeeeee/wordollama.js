# Code of conduct

WordOllama.JS aims to be a practical, respectful, and safe open-source project.
Participants must communicate in good faith, critique ideas and code rather than
people, respect privacy, and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, or sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, repeated behavior, and
the safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to liboyang@lslby.com. Include links or
screenshots where safe, but do not forward private documents or credentials.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical; retaliation against a
reporter or participant is not acceptable.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers,
review channels, and public events where someone is representing the project.
